🎲 TRIP OUT — Tips & Strategy
🎯 Scoring
Singles1 = 100 pts · 5 = 50 pts · 2, 3, 4, 6 = 0
Triples3×1 = 3,000 · 3×2 = 200 · 3×3 = 300 · 3×4 = 400 · 3×5 = 1,500 · 3×6 = 600
4-of-a-KindDoubles the triple · ×1=6k · ×2=400 · ×3=600 · ×4=800 · ×5=3k · ×6=1,200
5-of-a-KindTriples the quad · ×1=12k · ×2=800 · ×3=1.2k · ×4=1.6k · ×5=6k · ×6=2.4k · always PUSH
Straight1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6 = 1,500 pts + any scoring dice pts · PUSH
Full House3+2 same faces = 500 + triple score + pair pts · PUSH
⚡ Key Rules
🎲Triples only form from dice rolled together in the same roll — never built across rolls
🔄Re-Trip: Triple the same number again this turn = 2× base pts (3rd = 4×, and so on)
✨After tripling: extra 1s = 1,000ea · extra 5s = 500ea · all others = 100ea
🔥PUSH = all 5 dice score → roll all 5 again! 4-of-a-kind only pushes if the 5th die also scores
💥CRASH = nothing scores → lose all cash pts, turn ends
🔒First bank needs 1,000+ pts to unlock · after that, bank any time you've scored 1+ die
🏆 Winning
🎯First player to bank 20,000 pts wins
🔥0 → 20,000 in one turn = TRIP OUT — the ultimate flex!
🏆Triple 2, 3, 4, & 6 in one turn = Automatic Winner!
🧠 Strategy
Bank early when you hit 1,000+. A bird in the hand beats chasing a crash. Aim for 2,000+ if it feels safe.
Already tripled a number? Roll again — a 2nd triple doubles your pts, a 3rd quadruples them!
Straights and Full Houses give all 5 dice back — always ride those pushes.
Watch the leaders. If someone is closing in on 20,000, take more risks to keep up.
After a triple, every extra 1 you roll is worth 1,000 — keep rolling if you see 1s!
Cash points vanish on a crash. Never leave a big stack on the table when you can bank it.
